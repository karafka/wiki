Share Groups support is coming in a future version of Karafka. Until the feature is available, this page covers only the routing layer.

The share-group **routing layer** is already in place: you can declare `share_group` blocks in your routing (validated, introspectable via `Karafka::App.share_groups`, filterable via `--include_share_groups`/`--exclude_share_groups` and visible in `karafka info`), but running them raises `Karafka::Errors::ShareGroupsNotImplementedError` because consuming from share groups is not available yet. The [routing `defaults` block](Consumer-Groups-Routing#shared-defaults) does not apply to `share_group` topics.
